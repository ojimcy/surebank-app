Primary Logo Variations

Horizontal Logo

Best for: Website headers, business cards, letterheads, wide banners
Minimum width: 200px
Clear space: 1/2 icon height around logo

Vertical Logo

Best for: App splash screens, social media profiles, square formats, business cards
Minimum height: 300px
Clear space: 1/3 icon width around logo

Compact Logo

Best for: Navigation bars, small headers, email signatures, mobile apps
Minimum width: 140px
Clear space: 1/3 icon height around logo

Alternative Versions

Monochrome Version

Use when: Single color printing, embossing/debossing, simplified applications
Color: Primary blue (#0066A1) or any brand color

Reversed (White) Version

Use on: Dark backgrounds, photos with dark overlays, colored backgrounds
Ensure: Sufficient contrast with background (minimum 4.5:1 ratio)

Usage Guidelines

Horizontal Logo

  • Website headers and navigation
  • Email signatures
  • Business cards and letterheads
  • Wide promotional banners
  • Footer branding

Vertical Logo

  • App splash screens and loading screens
  • Social media profile images
  • Square format advertisements
  • Presentation title slides
  • Product packaging

Compact Logo

  • Mobile app navigation
  • Browser favicons
  • Small header spaces
  • Invoice and receipt headers
  • Tight space applications

Don't

  • Stretch or distort the logo
  • Change colors outside brand palette
  • Place on busy backgrounds without contrast
  • Use below minimum size requirements
  • Remove clear space around logo
